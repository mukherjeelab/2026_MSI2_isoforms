B. Analysis map and four questions

Q1 · Does CLIP-versus-SMI enrichment differ between constructs?

Separate paired DESeq2 → descriptive effect comparison → strict / positive-selected peaks → all-nonzero continuous preference → quantile-normalized sensitivity → round-two peak configurations.

A direct count-model construct × assay interaction remains PROPOSED / NOT YET RUN.

Q2 · Where is the difference?

Retained donor / acceptor / annotated-end windows → common-CPM site-weighted profiles → exon/intron side tests → coverage strata → expanded round-two windows and scaled intron/3′UTR coordinates.

Q3 · CLIP, SMI, or both?

Common-CPM profiles → arithmetic CLIP and SMI coverage + exact mean-log component decomposition. Round-two additive differences are a separate quantity.

Q4 · How robust is it?

Peak selection; full versus selected-library denominator; pseudocount; quantile normalization; equal-depth sampling; site versus pooled-read weighting; measurable-site masks; gene-stratified peak proximity.

Final synthesis

Stable observations

Both constructs have reproducibly enriched peak subsets. In the original all-nonzero and QN peak comparisons, the UTR-minus-intron preference difference stays negative and small. Under equal-site landmark weighting, 328 relative enrichment is stronger near exonic splice boundaries across the saved scale/pseudocount sensitivities.

Processing-sensitive observations

The overall magnitude is not stable. Outcome selection, measurable-site masks, pseudocount, selected-library denominator and log/replicate aggregation order all matter. Pooling reads can reverse the direction. Round-two coverage differences weaken substantially after peak selection.

Strongest spatial observations

Original common-CPM site-weighted donor and acceptor minima are on the exonic side. CLIP-only separation is visible there before SMI normalization. Transcript-end differences are broad and involve both components. Round-two genome-wide scaled profiles show different intron and 3′UTR coverage tendencies, which must be described as depth patterns.

Major technical limitations

Two biological pairs per construct, sparse CLIP, unequal library sizes, extensive zeros, pseudocount dependence, overlapping gene/feature models, selection effects, and a single sampling seed limit inference. Gene/site uncertainty does not replace biological-replicate uncertainty. Peak significance in one construct alone does not prove an interaction, and these analyses do not establish altered splicing.

Open analyses

The direct construct × assay count interaction is proposed, not run. Source extraction workflows and the historical joint-positive generator are not fully reconstructable locally. Site-distribution and gene-stratified proximity diagnostics are completed; undefined sparse-stratum intervals are explicitly retained.

Immediate grant-use options

Use the donor or acceptor component panels to communicate the localization hypothesis while making the SMI contribution visible. Pair them with weighting sensitivity. Use the original/QN continuous pair to communicate the modest region effect honestly. Include a support-distribution or peak-proximity companion when describing how broadly the spatial observation applies. Choose based on the claim to communicate, not the largest visual separation.
