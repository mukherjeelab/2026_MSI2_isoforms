# 2026_MSI2_isoforms
Repository containing data and code for 2026 MSI2 paper.

## Round-two read and feature comparisons

All ten original-library analysis variants, continuous peak preference, expanded
landmark metaplots, and standardized intron/3′UTR profiles are compared in
[`output/round2_comparisons/report.html`](output/round2_comparisons/report.html).
Methods and interpretation are in
[`output/round2_comparisons/README.md`](output/round2_comparisons/README.md);
rerun with `Rscript analysis/compare_round2.R`.

## CLIP peak-enrichment analysis

The fixed-peak featureCounts and paired DESeq2 workflow is documented in
[`code/clip_peak_analysis/README.md`](code/clip_peak_analysis/README.md).
Downloaded count, DESeq2, and QC outputs are under
[`data/results/peak_analysis/`](data/results/peak_analysis/); their contents and
current interpretation are summarized in
[`data/results/peak_analysis/README.md`](data/results/peak_analysis/README.md).
The standalone exploratory visualization workflows are
[`analysis/visualize_peak_deseq.R`](analysis/visualize_peak_deseq.R) and the
all-nonzero-peak PCA, heatmap, and CLIP/SMI comparison in
[`analysis/visualize_all_nonzero_peaks.R`](analysis/visualize_all_nonzero_peaks.R).
The exploratory transcript-region comparison of construct-biased peaks is in
[`analysis/analyze_construct_biased_peak_regions.R`](analysis/analyze_construct_biased_peak_regions.R).
That script preserves the older threshold-based delta-group analysis. The
primary continuous construct-preference analysis, which uses all 16,046 peaks
with any raw count and tests delta-delta differences between transcript-region
annotations without a direction-dependent filter, is in
[`analysis/analyze_continuous_construct_preference.R`](analysis/analyze_continuous_construct_preference.R).
A separate quantile-normalized log2-CPM sensitivity analysis of the same peak
universe is generated by
[`analysis/analyze_quantile_normalized_construct_preference.R`](analysis/analyze_quantile_normalized_construct_preference.R).

## Landmark enrichment first-pass analysis

The site-weighted MSI2-324 versus MSI2-328 CLIP/SMI landmark analysis is in
[`analysis/analyze_landmark_splicing_first_pass.R`](analysis/analyze_landmark_splicing_first_pass.R).
Its validated first-pass tables, PDF/PNG figures, methods, and concise findings
are under [`output/splicing_first_pass/`](output/splicing_first_pass/).

## Common-CPM landmark investigation

The primary-mapped-read CPM reanalysis, replicate regional estimates,
side-of-splice tests, and condition-independent coverage stratification are in
[`analysis/investigate_landmark_normalization_and_coverage.R`](analysis/investigate_landmark_normalization_and_coverage.R).
The separate result bundle and first report are under
[`output/splicing_common_cpm_investigation/`](output/splicing_common_cpm_investigation/);
the earlier first-pass outputs are retained unchanged for QC comparison.

The additive CLIP-only and SMI-only component diagnostic is in
[`analysis/diagnose_clip_smi_components.R`](analysis/diagnose_clip_smi_components.R),
with its four-panel comparison figures, replicate QC, exact log-component
decomposition, and interpretation under
[`output/splicing_clip_smi_diagnostic/`](output/splicing_clip_smi_diagnostic/).
