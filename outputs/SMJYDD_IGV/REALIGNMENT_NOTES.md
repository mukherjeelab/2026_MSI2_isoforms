# L2 against 346: alternative alignment allowing larger deletions

Open `L2_on_346_MSI2_compare_realignments.xml` in IGV using File > Open Session. It loads both L2 samples with separate alternative and original tracks, at `346_MSI2:650-887`. All resource/index paths are absolute paths on this Mac.

All 57 HEK and 158 H295R previously identified 63-base-deletion reads now contain a 120D operation in the alternative alignment to 346. The downstream aligned sequence is recovered. This represents the 63-base deletion relative to 328 plus the absence of the 57-base segment present in 346. It does not establish a new independent 120-base biological event. Exact synthetic 346, 328, and 328-minus-63 sequences produce 887M, 751M57D79M, and 688M120D79M, respectively.

The original BAMs and all count/variant classifications are unchanged. The new BAMs retain exactly the same read set and mapped/unmapped status as their original 346 views: 1,431 HEK records (1,323 mapped) and 1,038 H295R records (780 mapped). Reads are >=500 nt. No discarded or previously unmapped reads are newly called mapped here.

Method: Biopython 1.83 PairwiseAligner semiglobal alignment of the complete oriented read against the complete 346 reference. Match +2, mismatch -4, affine gap opening -12, extension -0.2, free end gaps on both sequences. Unaligned terminal read sequence remains soft-clipped; this intentionally does not force adapters/unrelated terminal sequence into the alignment. Long internal gaps are less costly than in the original minimap2 view. Only originally mapped reads were realigned, preserving their established strand. An alternative replaces the original only if it retains at least 95% of the original matching-base count and at least 85% identity in paired base columns. This is an exploratory alignment model, not a revised quantitative genotype analysis.

Alternative alignments have MAPQ 255 (not available), because minimap2 MAPQ cannot be reused after realignment. NM and MD were recomputed and independently checked against the actual reference and read sequences. AS is the alternative score multiplied by ten to preserve fractional gap penalties; it is not comparable to the original AS. Tag ZA identifies the method; original competitive classification and locus tags ZC/ZL are preserved and have not been recomputed.

The sequence, quality and read names are preserved, with reverse-strand orientation as in the original BAM. CIGAR lengths, NM, MD reconstruction, unique names, minimum read length, BAM integrity and indexed retrieval were validated. All 215 known deletion-supporting reads have at least 20 aligned bases downstream of the combined gap. See semiglobal_validation.txt and semiglobal_changes.tsv for details. The sessions were generated and file paths checked but not visually tested because computer-control access was unavailable.

Script: `analysis/realign_l2_igv.py` (system Python with project-local Biopython). It writes alternative SAMs to `tmp/SMJYDD_IGV_SAM`; the delivered BAMs were sorted/indexed using project-local pysam 0.24.1 under bundled Python 3.12. The comparison session includes both original and new alignments; do not sum reads across the tracks.
