# Exploratory construct-biased peak regions

This parent directory contains the **older discrete, threshold-based** analysis
of simplified transcript-region annotations for three delta-enrichment peak
groups. The CLIP/SMI ratios and cutoffs are descriptive; this is not a DESeq2
interaction test of construct specificity.

The **primary continuous construct-preference analysis** is now in
`continuous_construct_preference/` and is generated by
`analysis/analyze_continuous_construct_preference.R`. It uses all 16,046 merged
peaks with any raw count, applies no delta-delta or preferred-construct cutoff,
and asks whether continuous delta-delta differs *between* the five transcript
regions. Its tests are an overall Kruskal-Wallis test, pairwise Wilcoxon
rank-sum tests with BH correction, and pairwise two-sample KS tests with BH
correction. The discrete files described below are retained unchanged for
provenance and secondary exploration.

## Peak groups

The grouping universe is the 16,046 merged peaks with a count in at least one
of the eight libraries. Delta is `324WT replicate-mean LFC - 328WT
replicate-mean LFC`.

- 324-biased: delta >= 1 and 324WT LFC > 0 (1,437 peaks).
- 328-biased: delta <= -1 and 328WT LFC > 0 (7,034 peaks).
- Similar-positive: absolute delta < 1 and both LFCs > 0 (717 peaks).
- Other: all remaining peaks (6,858 peaks).

The Similar-positive definition requires positive enrichment in both
constructs so peaks that are similarly depleted are not interpreted as shared
binding.

## Transcript-region annotation

Each merged peak is represented by the summit of its highest-scoring source
omniCLIP peak. Each unmerged source peak uses its own summit. Both are annotated
strand-specifically against `TxDb.Hsapiens.UCSC.hg38.knownGene` version 3.20.0.

The peak-to-region assignments are cached in
`peak_transcript_region_annotations.tsv` and
`source_peak_transcript_region_annotations.tsv`. Subsequent runs validate row
counts, identifiers, and required columns and load these tables directly. The
genomic overlaps are rebuilt only when either cache is absent or invalid.

The reported categories are `5' UTR`, `CDS`, `3' UTR`, `Intron`, and `Other`.
`Other` combines ambiguous UTR/CDS annotations, other exon/noncoding
annotations, intergenic peaks, and any remaining categories. The detailed
annotation is retained in the annotation tables.

## Backgrounds

Two full backgrounds are reported without subsampling:

1. The primary matched-unit background contains all 43,794 merged peak
   regions. It uses the same merged-row unit as the selected delta groups.
2. The requested sensitivity background contains all 46,108 source BED peaks
   with column 5 score >= 1. These are unmerged source rows, so comparisons use
   their proportions as fixed expected rates rather than pretending the source
   rows and selected merged rows are independent samples of the same unit.

The backgrounds are nearly identical: the merged versus source distributions
are 59.91% versus 59.86% 3' UTR, 29.38% versus 29.38% intron, 4.18% versus
4.18% CDS, 0.36% versus 0.37% 5' UTR, and 6.17% versus 6.21% Other. Merging has
negligible effect on the reference proportions.

The older 16,046 counted-peak distribution is retained in
`transcript_region_background.tsv` for sensitivity and provenance, but it is
not the primary background.

## Main results

All three selected groups are strongly shifted toward 3' UTR and away from
intron compared with the full original peak landscape. Against the primary
43,794 merged-peak background:

- 324-biased peaks are 84.20% 3' UTR versus 59.91% background (1.405-fold,
  BH-adjusted Fisher p = 3.47e-91). CDS is 6.26% versus 4.18% (1.499-fold,
  adjusted p = 2.70e-4). Intron is 5.08% versus 29.38% (0.173-fold, adjusted
  p = 1.76e-123).
- 328-biased peaks are 88.90% 3' UTR (1.484-fold) and 2.80% intron
  (0.095-fold); both adjusted p-values are below the numerical reporting limit.
  CDS is 4.02% versus 4.18% and is not significant (adjusted p = 0.495).
- Similar-positive peaks are 91.21% 3' UTR (1.522-fold, adjusted p = 2.80e-80),
  1.67% CDS (0.401-fold, adjusted p = 3.69e-4), and 2.09% intron (0.071-fold,
  adjusted p = 4.05e-84).

The 46,108-source-peak sensitivity analysis gives the same fold-enrichment
pattern because its background proportions are almost unchanged.

These large selected-versus-full-background effects should not be read as
construct specificity by themselves. Selection into a delta group requires
measurable CLIP/SMI signal, and counted peaks are already much more 3'
UTR-weighted than the complete peak catalog. The direct group comparisons are
therefore more informative for differences between constructs.

## Direct comparisons among delta groups

The three-group region distribution differs overall (Pearson chi-square =
54.89, df = 8, Monte Carlo p < 1e-5, Cramer's V = 0.055). This is statistically
strong but globally small.

For 324-biased versus 328-biased peaks, the overall difference remains
significant after collapsing to five categories (chi-square = 36.72, df = 4,
Monte Carlo p < 1e-5, Cramer's V = 0.066). The region-level drivers are:

- 3' UTR: 84.20% versus 88.90%, 324/328 fold = 0.947, adjusted p = 6.44e-6.
- CDS: 6.26% versus 4.02%, fold = 1.557, adjusted p = 5.06e-4.
- Intron: 5.08% versus 2.80%, fold = 1.814, adjusted p = 5.76e-5.
- Other and 5' UTR do not differ after BH correction.

The Similar-positive group is more 3' UTR-focused and has less CDS than the
324-biased group. Their overall difference has Cramer's V = 0.130. CDS, 3' UTR,
and intron pass the within-pair BH correction. The 328-biased versus
Similar-positive contrast is smaller (Cramer's V = 0.041; Monte Carlo
BH-adjusted p = 0.0144); only CDS passes the per-region BH correction (4.02%
versus 1.67%, 2.402-fold, adjusted p = 0.00473).

## Continuous delta-delta figure

`delta_LFC_by_transcript_region.pdf` uses the requested relaxed filter without
assigning discrete groups. A peak is retained when either condition holds:

- delta >= 0 and 324WT enrichment > 0
- delta <= 0 and 328WT enrichment > 0

This is equivalent to requiring the more enriched construct to have positive
CLIP/SMI enrichment. Peaks depleted relative to SMI in both constructs are
removed. There are no exact delta = 0 ties, so the two conditions do not
overlap. The filter retains 9,369 peaks and plots the continuous quantity:

`delta-delta = 324WT log2(CLIP/SMI) - 328WT log2(CLIP/SMI)`

Each construct's LFC is calculated from mean CPM-normalized counts as
`log2((mean CLIP CPM + 0.5)/(mean SMI CPM + 0.5))`. Positive delta-delta values
favor 324WT and negative values favor 328WT. Across the retained peaks, the
median delta-delta is -3.859 and the paired Wilcoxon p-value is below R's
floating-point reporting range. The median 324WT and 328WT enrichment values
are 0 and 3.400, respectively.

Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests were also run within each transcript region,
with BH correction across the five categories. All five regions are shifted
below zero: 5' UTR n = 28, median = -4.512, q = 0.0043; CDS n = 398, median =
-3.893, q = 2.89e-26; 3' UTR n = 8,271, median = -3.859, q < 1e-300; intron
n = 290, median = -3.644, q = 1.61e-8; and Other n = 382, median = -3.644,
q = 1.99e-30.

The broad negative shift should be interpreted with the library imbalance in
mind. Of the retained peaks, 7,571 have negative delta and 1,798 have positive
delta. There are 6,420 peaks with positive 328WT enrichment but non-positive
324WT enrichment, compared with 935 in the reverse direction; 2,014 are
positive in both constructs.

## Statistical tests

- Primary overall background tests use a 2 x 5 Pearson chi-square table for
  each selected group versus all remaining merged peaks. Monte Carlo p-values
  use 100,000 replicates. Per-region tests use two-sided Fisher exact tests and
  BH correction across five regions within each group.
- Source-background sensitivity tests use a Pearson goodness-of-fit test
  against the fixed 46,108-source-peak proportions. Per-region tests use exact
  binomial tests and BH correction across five regions within each group.
- Direct tests include one 3 x 5 Pearson chi-square test and all three pairwise
  2 x 5 chi-square tests. Pairwise region tests use two-sided Fisher tests with
  BH correction across five regions within each pair.

## Files

- `construct_bias_peak_groups.tsv`: 16,046 counted peaks with LFCs, delta,
  simplified and detailed annotations, exploratory group definitions, and the
  Boolean relaxed-filter flag used for the continuous density plot.
- `peak_transcript_region_annotations.tsv`: representative-summit annotations
  for all 43,794 merged peak regions.
- `source_peak_transcript_region_annotations.tsv`: summit annotations for all
  46,108 source BED peaks.
- `transcript_region_background.tsv`: source, merged, and counted-background
  percentages in the five simplified categories.
- `transcript_region_enrichment.tsv`: descriptive percentages and fold
  enrichment for all three delta groups against all three backgrounds.
- `delta_group_vs_merged_background_tests.tsv`: primary per-region Fisher tests.
- `delta_group_vs_source_background_tests.tsv`: requested source-background
  exact-binomial sensitivity tests.
- `overall_region_distribution_chisquare.tsv` and
  `source_background_goodness_of_fit_chisquare.tsv`: overall background tests.
- `three_group_overall_chisquare.tsv`: direct three-group distribution test.
- `pairwise_group_overall_chisquare.tsv` and
  `pairwise_group_region_fisher.tsv`: all direct pairwise tests.
- `direct_324_vs_328_overall_chisquare.tsv` and
  `direct_324_vs_328_region_fisher.tsv`: compatibility subsets of the pairwise
  tables.
- `delta_group_peak_selection.pdf`: scatter plot of all three delta groups.
- `construct_bias_transcript_region_enrichment.pdf`: five-category enrichment
  against the matched merged and requested source backgrounds.
- `delta_LFC_by_transcript_region.pdf`: continuous delta distributions across
  the five categories after applying the relaxed positive-preferred filter;
  paired regional statistics are printed above the distributions.
- `relaxed_filter_continuous_delta_delta_peaks.tsv`: the 9,369 peak-level
  values used by the continuous delta-delta figure.
- `relaxed_filter_overall_paired_test.tsv`: the overall paired Wilcoxon result
  for the relaxed-filter figure.
- The older `jointly_CLIP_enriched_*` TSVs are retained as a record of the
  previous both-constructs-positive filter; they are not inputs to the current
  density figure.
- `strict_quadrant_peak_selection.pdf`: retained strict opposite-quadrant view.
- `analysis_parameters.tsv`: exact thresholds, backgrounds, and test definitions.
